Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Watch Together is a small website for watching videos in sync with friends and chatting while you watch. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to have it removed.

What we store

DataWhy
Your username and email addressTo create your account and let you sign in. Your email is not shown to other users.
Your password, stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt)To check your password at sign-in. Nobody, including us, can read the original password.
If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, email and profile picture linkTo create or find your account. We ask Google only for your basic profile and email, nothing else.
Rooms you create, chat messages, reactions, and the video links played in a room (with their public titles and thumbnails)So the room works: other people in the room see the chat, reactions and video history.
Technical session data (a session cookie and a login token kept in your browser)To keep you signed in. You can remove them by logging out or clearing your browser data.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Where it is stored

The website runs on Back4app. The data above is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database hosted in the European Union (Paris).

Other services the website uses

  • Video players from YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion or the site a video link points to. When a video plays, that service receives your connection like any embedded video and applies its own privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In, only if you choose it.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs, which deliver the website's fonts and icons to your browser.

How long we keep it

Your account and what you post stay until you ask us to delete them. Video players and other services keep their own data according to their policies.

Your choices

  • You can log out at any time, which removes the login data from your browser.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the messages you posted.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Watch Together's access in your Google Account settings, under Security → Third-party connections.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue on the project's page: github.com/mel-mehdi/watch_together/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
